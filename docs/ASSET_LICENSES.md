## Procedural 3D surfaces

Oak, walnut, ash, plaster, linen, bouclé, velvet, travertine, and the rug are painted onto canvases in `components/three/roomMaterials.ts` when the studio loads. They are not sampled from a photograph. Nothing is downloaded, and nothing needs attribution.

## Not used

No GLB, HDR, environment map, or stock photograph is referenced. If photography is added later, record the file path, author, source URL, license, and whether attribution must be shown. Reference photos have to be labeled as inspiration, not as completed SPACE projects.
